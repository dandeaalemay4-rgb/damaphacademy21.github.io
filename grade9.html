Grade 9 Curriculum

Comprehensive units and study resources for Grade 9 Mathematics and Physics.

📐 Mathematics Units (9 Units)

Unit 1: Further on Sets

Set structure, classification, operations, and real-life applications.

Unit 2: The Number System

Rational, irrational, real numbers, integers, and fractions.

Unit 3: Solving Equations

Linear and non-linear equations, algebraic rules.

Unit 4: Solving Inequalities

Linear, absolute value, quadratic inequalities, and graphing.

Unit 5: Introduction to Trigonometry

Trigonometric ratios, right-angled triangles, geometry.

Unit 6: Regular Polygons

Interior/exterior angles, properties, and patterns.

Unit 7: Congruency and Similarity

Geometric theorems, ratios of sides and areas.

Unit 8: Vectors in Two Dimensions

Vector vs scalar, operations, magnitude, and direction.

Unit 9: Statistics and Probability

Data collection, interpretation, probability, and predictions.



🔭 Physics Units (7 Units)

Unit 1: Physics and Human Society

Branches of physics, relationship to other fields, prominent physicists.

Unit 2: Physical Quantities

Scales, standards, units, measurement, and unit conversion.

Unit 3: Motion in a Straight Line

Position, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration, graphs.

Unit 4: Force, Work, Energy, and Power

Newton's laws, friction, kinetic/potential energy, power.

Unit 5: Simple Machines

Mechanical advantage, velocity ratio, efficiency, types of machines.

Unit 6: Mechanical Oscillation & Waves

Wave characteristics, sound waves, superposition, oscillations.

Unit 7: Temperature and Thermometry

Temperature scales, thermometers, thermal expansion.
