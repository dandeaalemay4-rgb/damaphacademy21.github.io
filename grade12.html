Grade 12 Curriculum

Comprehensive units and study resources for Grade 12 Mathematics and Physics.

📐 Mathematics Units (5 Units)

Unit 1: Sequences and Series

Arithmetic and geometric progressions, infinite series, convergence, and mathematical induction.

Unit 2: Introduction to Calculus

Limits of functions, continuity, intuitive concepts of derivatives, and rates of change.

Unit 3: Differential Calculus

Rules of differentiation, chain rule, implicit differentiation, and applications of derivatives.

Unit 4: Integral Calculus

Indefinite integrals, integration techniques, definite integrals, and area under curves.

Unit 5: Three Dimensional Geometry & Vectors

Planes in 3D space, lines in 3D, vector products, and distance formulas in 3D.



🔭 Physics Units (5 Units)

Unit 1: Application of Physics in Other Fields

Physics in medicine, engineering, technology, environmental science, and interdisciplinary fields.

Unit 2: Two-Dimensional Motion

Vectors in 2D, projectile motion, circular motion dynamics, and relative motion in two dimensions.

Unit 3: Fluid Mechanics

Fluid statics, fluid dynamics, Bernoulli's equation, viscosity, and practical applications.

Unit 4: Electromagnetism

Magnetic force, Faraday's law of induction, Lenz's law, Maxwell's equations, and electromagnetic radiation.

Unit 5: Atomic Physics

Atomic models, photoelectric effect, quantum concepts, energy levels, and nuclear phenomena.
