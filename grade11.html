Grade 11 Curriculum

Comprehensive units and study resources for Grade 11 Mathematics and Physics.

📐 Mathematics Units (8 Units)

Unit 1: Reasoning and Logic

Mathematical logic, truth tables, logical connectives, tautologies, and methods of proof.

Unit 2: Rational Expressions & Functions

Simplifying rational expressions, partial fractions, domain, asymptotes, and graphing.

Unit 3: Matrices and Determinants

Matrix operations, inverse matrices, determinants, and solving systems of equations.

Unit 4: Further Trigonometry

Trigonometric identities, compound angle formulas, double angle formulas, and equations.

Unit 5: Transformations of the Plane

Translation, rotation, reflection, dilation, and matrix transformation models.

Unit 6: Statistics and Probability

Measures of central tendency, dispersion, probability distributions, and combinations.

Unit 7: Vectors in 3D

3D coordinate systems, dot product, cross product, and vector equations of lines/planes.

Unit 8: Complex Numbers

Real and imaginary parts, modulus, argument, polar form, and Operations on complex numbers.



🔭 Physics Units (7 Units)

Unit 1: Physics and Measurement

Physical quantities, dimensional analysis, measurement uncertainty, and error propagation.

Unit 2: Two-Dimensional Motion

Projectile motion, uniform circular motion, angular displacement, and centripetal force.

Unit 3: Fluid Mechanics

Fluid dynamics, Bernoulli's principle, viscosity, surface tension, and practical applications.

Unit 4: Electromagnetism

Magnetic field lines, Faraday's law, Lenz's law, inductors, and electromagnetic waves.

Unit 5: Basic Electronics

Semiconductors, p-n junctions, diodes, rectifiers, transistors, and simple circuits.

Unit 6: Mechanical Waves

Transverse and longitudinal waves, wave equation, interference, diffraction, and standing waves.

Unit 7: Geometric Optics

Reflection, refraction, Snell's law, thin lens formula, mirrors, and optical instruments.
