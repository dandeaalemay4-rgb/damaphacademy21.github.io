Grade 10 Curriculum

Comprehensive units and study resources for Grade 10 Mathematics and Physics.

📐 Mathematics Units (7 Units)

Unit 1: Relations and Functions

Concept of relations, domain and range, functional notation, and special types of functions.

Unit 2: Polynomial Functions

Operations on polynomials, roots of polynomials, synthetic division, and graphing.

Unit 3: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

Properties of exponents, logarithmic identities, solving exponential equations, and graphs.

Unit 4: Trigonometric Functions

Unit circle, radian measure, trigonometric identities, and periodic functions.

Unit 5: Circles

Circle equations, tangents, secants, arc lengths, sector areas, and geometric theorems.

Unit 6: Solid Figures

Surface area and volume of prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, and spheres.

Unit 7: Coordinate Geometry

Distance formula, midpoint formula, slope of lines, parallel and perpendicular lines.



🔭 Physics Units (6 Units)

Unit 1: Vector Quantities

Representation of vectors, resolution of vectors, vector addition, and scalar products.

Unit 2: Uniformly Accelerated Motion

Kinematic equations, free-fall motion, graphical analysis, and relative velocity.

Unit 3: Elasticity and Static Equilibrium

Hooke's law, stress and strain, Young's modulus, conditions for static equilibrium, and torque.

Unit 4: Static and Flowing Fluids

Fluid pressure, Pascal's principle, Archimedes' principle, buoyancy, and continuity equation.

Unit 5: Thermal Energy

Heat capacity, specific heat capacity, latent heat, and thermal conduction.

Unit 6: Electrostatics and Magnetism

Coulomb's law, electric fields, magnetic fields, magnetic force, and electromagnetic induction.
